About Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is a simple, focused app for building better routines. Add the habits you care about, then watch your streaks grow each day you show up.

There is no clutter and nothing to configure — just your habits and the momentum you build. The app ships pre-loaded with a few example habits so you always have a starting point.

  • Track daily habits and streaks
  • Add new habits in a single tap
  • Stay motivated with fire-streak counters
